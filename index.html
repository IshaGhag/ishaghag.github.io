About Me 🌸

Hey! I'm Isha — a Computer Science postgraduate from Mumbai with an MSc (8.39 CGPA) and BSc (9.00 CGPA) from Thakur College, Mumbai University.

I love building things that are practical and fun to use. Whether it's a web app that helps people cook better at home or a tool that saves hours of reading time — I care about the end experience.

My work lives at the crossroads of frontend development and data intelligence. Getting into NLP was a turning point — realising machines can actually understand meaning, not just text.

When I'm not coding, I'm curious about Edge AI — the idea that smart models can run right where the data is, without a cloud in sight.

I'm actively looking for developer or analyst roles in Mumbai where I can jump in and contribute from day one!
